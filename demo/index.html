jt-water

Motion as physics, not easing curves. Everything below runs on one shared field: critically damped glides, viscous coasting, decaying ripples.

1 · A highlight bar glides between blocks

Click a paragraph. The bar moves through the medium: fast entry, drag, gentle arrival, zero overshoot.

Reading is a slow art. The eye moves, pauses, returns; attention pools in one place before it flows to the next.

An interface that respects this does not snap. It travels — the way a thing underwater travels — quickly at first, then braked by the medium, arriving without a bounce.

Nothing here is a timing curve chosen by hand. The trajectory falls out of a damped equation of motion, so every element in jt moves the same way because they share the same water.

2 · Drag, release, and it settles like an object in water

Throw the pebble. On release it coasts against viscous drag to a predictable resting point — no bounce, no spring-back.

3 · A disturbance ripples the neighbors

Click a stone. Energy radiates outward and strictly decays; near stones move most, far ones less and later.
