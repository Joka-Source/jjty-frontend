# JJTY Sage design edition

The founder's 12 September brief authorizes creative decisions and prioritizes a complete designer prototype over operational integrations. This is a visual, clickable edition with fictional sample content. No mail, microphone, account, or document backend is connected.

## Direction

A calm working desk with precise controls and small, tactile moments. PostHog's desktop-like framing and approachable product density inspire the chrome; the existing Mobbin selection informs recent work, context-preserving composition, reading controls, capture, and recovery. Original JJTY screens and illustrations are authored here. No third-party design ownership is implied.

Palette: Mist #F3F6EF, Paper #FCFDF9, Sage #D9E8CE, Forest #294C3B, Ink #25382C, Lavender #E6DFF3, Butter #F1E8B8. Dark uses #17251F and #203128 with pale sage actions. Light and dark are equally composed. Product text is a humanist sans; editorial headings use Georgia. Borders organize panes; rounded corners distinguish cards, dialogs, and paper.

## Experience

Home provides recent work, a featured document, quick capture, and activity. Library, Inbox, Reader, Notebook, and Capture are actual designed sample screens. A journey atlas covers all 27 inherited journey IDs; each sequence renders the relevant product screen and step-specific content, with normal, empty, offline, and error treatments. A bottom walkthrough bar exposes progress and controls without confusing prototype progress with backend success.

The design system includes palette, type, controls, states, and theme previews. Reference links retain provenance. Sample data is visibly labeled at the application level; send and account actions remain explicitly prototype-only at confirmation.

## Acceptance

Every catalog journey has a routed, populated walkthrough and final state. Light/dark themes persist. Search/filter, walkthrough navigation, dialogs, and main workspace routes respond. Desktop and 390px layouts must be visually inspected. No production, native, provider, or store claim follows from this pass.

## Widget and advanced-mode direction

Widgets use generous concentric corners, paper/sage surfaces, a single dominant value or object, and no miniature dashboards. Resume, Capture, and a medium recent-item/quick-action widget form the initial family. MacroFactor, Shop, and Mesh specimens inspected in Mobbin informed information hierarchy, not copied artwork. Exact links are preserved on the Widgets & islands page.

The upper deep-ink capsule represents ongoing activity (listening, reading, saving), with compact and expanded presentations. The lower frosted-sage Jelly presents contextual next actions. Only one expands at a time. Advanced mode is opt-in in this prototype. Use a restrained 320ms transition, reduced-motion crossfade, and no idle bouncing; waveform motion indicates a sample listening state. Completion is inspectable in this concept; real runtime dismissal, keyboard avoidance, and native safe-area handling are integration requirements.

On supported iOS devices, the upper system surface requires Live Activities/ActivityKit. The lower Jelly is an in-app control, not a second system Dynamic Island. This browser concept does not implement either native API. Apple guidance: https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10194/.
