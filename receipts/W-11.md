## Outcome

PDF sources retain their bytes for rendering, open as real PDF.js page canvases with selectable text
layers, zoom from 75% to 250%, and provide next/previous in-document search with a visible hit count
and an explicit no-match result. Existing act highlights are re-resolved after each zoom swap, while
the rendered page being read remains in place until its replacement is ready.

The durable address remains the jt-core quote-plus-context match. Position remains a cache. Normalized
PDF page geometry is stored only as fallback/audit evidence and is never consulted by anchor resolution.

Encrypted, corrupt, and image-only PDFs have distinct refusal states. Source bytes and provenance are
retained even when opening is refused; an image-only PDF can still render its pages while saying that
there is no text layer.

`src/db.js` and the verb registry have no diff.
