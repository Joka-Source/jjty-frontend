jt

you open a page and read it aloud. the page follows your voice, and when you say what you want — “highlight this”, “mark this important”, “note that…” — it happens, right where you are.

every act leaves a record you can open, check, and undo. if jt is not sure what you meant, it asks — it never guesses. and everything stays on this device unless you choose to send it.

one question

jt works by listening while you read. your browser will ask for the microphone once — say yes, and from then on speaking is the interface. jt keeps no recordings; your words are handled the moment they are spoken and let go.

you can turn voice on any time from settings.

your documents

    files are read here, on this device — each document keeps its fingerprint (digest, size) so you always know what you opened.

    what happened

    every act — spoken or tapped — leaves a record with its evidence. undo is itself a record, so the trail never thins.

      sharing

      jt pairs two devices with three spoken words — words you can say across a room, over a call, or into the other device’s mic. no codes to squint at, nothing typed twice. what travels is the whole moment: the passage, the act, its evidence, and the document’s fingerprint.

      share with another device

      forgetting ends this connection on both devices. arrived moments and saved outbox items stay on this device.

      arrived from other devices

        nothing has arrived yet. once paired, anything the other device sends lands here.

        what does “verified” mean?

        when a moment arrives, jt recomputes its content fingerprint and compares it with the one the sender wrote. if they match, the badge says verified: what you see is exactly what was sent, untouched in transit. if they don’t, jt says so instead of pretending.

        spaces PROVISIONAL · early — the shape may change

        jt adopts the real structure of your life at an institution — your college, your batch, your class, your hostel room — instead of inventing one.

        space feeds are local to this device for now. cross-device space sync arrives with the relay; this early surface does not pretend it is here yet.
